# Contributing to Robsonwood Corps

Robsonwood Corps develops software, research, and engineering projects across a variety of disciplines.

We welcome constructive technical discussion and contributions to projects that explicitly accept them.

## Contribution Guidelines

- **Respect project boundaries.** Each repository defines its own license, contribution requirements, and development scope.
- **Document your work.** Explain changes, their purpose, relevant dependencies, and any known limitations.
- **Validate your changes.** Test where practical and clearly distinguish verified results from untested assumptions.
- **Respect intellectual property.** Submit only material you have the right to contribute. Observe upstream licenses and attribution requirements.
- **Protect confidential information.** Never submit credentials, private data, proprietary source material, or unpublished research without authorization.
- **Prioritize engineering quality.** Favour maintainability, interoperability, reproducibility, and thoughtful design.

For significant changes, please open an issue to discuss the proposed approach before submitting a pull request.

## Licensing and Ownership

Licensing is determined individually by each project. The existence of a public repository does not imply permission to reuse its contents beyond the rights granted by its applicable license.

Robsonwood Corps maintains both publicly released and privately developed projects. Public contribution guidelines do not authorize access to confidential or proprietary work.
