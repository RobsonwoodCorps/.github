# Robsonwood Corps

### Engineered in the North.

**Independent engineering. Rooted in nature. Driven by curiosity.**

Robsonwood Corps is an independent technology and engineering initiative exploring the intersection of software, hardware, and intelligent systems.

Inspired by the Canadian Rockies, we believe technology should empower the people who use it. Our work combines practical engineering, experimentation, and a commitment to computational independence.

---

### Areas of Exploration

- **Native & Systems Software** — High-performance applications, creative tools, platform integration, and systems development.
- **Artificial Intelligence** — Local inference, autonomous systems, intelligent applications, and privacy-conscious computing.
- **Reverse Engineering & Compatibility** — Understanding complex systems, improving interoperability, and extending technological capabilities.
- **Hardware & Architecture** — Exploring the relationship between physical computing, software, and emerging architectures.
- **Research & Product Development** — From experimental prototypes and open-source contributions to independently developed software products.

### Our Philosophy

We believe meaningful innovation comes from curiosity, independence, and understanding how things actually work.

Our guiding principles:

- **Independence** — Technology should serve its users, not the other way around.
- **Privacy** — User ownership, local control, and thoughtful data practices matter.
- **Craftsmanship** — Good engineering deserves attention to detail.
- **Exploration** — Question assumptions. Understand limitations. Build beyond them.
- **Freedom to Create** — Technology should expand possibilities, not unnecessarily constrain them.

### Our Work

Robsonwood Corps encompasses independent research, software development, technical experimentation, and product engineering.

We contribute to the broader engineering community through selected open-source projects, published research, and collaborative development.

Each publicly released project defines its own scope, licensing, and contribution terms.
